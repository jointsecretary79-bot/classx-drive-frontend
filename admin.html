Post an update

Adds an entry to the Daily updates log on the app, and optionally to the yellow ticker. Photos and PDFs are uploaded straight into the GitHub repo.

What happened

Link and files

Pick as many as you like. Photos show as a tappable, zoomable gallery; PDFs get their own “Open PDF” button. Every file is stamped with today’s date and time. If you don’t set a link, the update points to the first file.

Where it shows

Check at least “ticker” or “log”, or the update won’t be visible. You can change all of this later in Posted updates.

Send notification

Broadcasts a push notification to everyone who has enabled updates.

Banners & videos

Images and videos shown on the home screen above the notification strip. Several items slide automatically; a playing video holds its slide until it finishes.

Add to the home screen

Choose what you’re adding, then check the preview on the right.

Best size: landscape 16:9 (e.g. 1280×720). Large images are shrunk automatically before upload.

MP4 (H.264) plays on every phone. Up to 30 MB — short clips load fastest. For longer videos, host them elsewhere and use “Video link”.

A direct link to an .mp4 or .webm file. Page links (YouTube, Instagram) won’t play here.

Make it as a 16:9 frame (like the Dussehra film) — it plays inside the same 16:9 home-screen frame, so it works for any festival or event. Single self-contained file, up to 2 MB. It restarts each time its slide comes into view, so set “Show for” to the length of its animation.

Shown before the video plays. For uploaded videos, the first frame is used if you leave this empty.

Photo & video saver

Bring in a photo or video you copied from another website, then download it or send it straight to a home banner or an update.

Paste or drop it here Press Ctrl+V (⌘V on Mac) after copying a photo, or drag a photo or video in from another tab.

Use a direct link to the file: right-click the photo or video on the site and choose “Copy image address” or “Copy video address”. Only save media you have permission to reuse.

Proxy for blocked sites

Many sites don’t let other web pages download their files. If you run your own server that fetches a link and returns it with CORS enabled, enter its address here and blocked links will go through it.

The link you paste is added to the end, URL-encoded. Leave blank to download directly.

Remove a user

Type a student’s email to block that profile. A blocked Gmail can’t sign in or register again, and is signed out the next time they open the site. You can restore anyone below.

GitHub connection

One connection powers posting, banners, folders and file management. It’s saved in this browser only.

Create it at github.com → Settings → Developer settings → Fine-grained tokens. Limit it to this one repo with “Contents: Read and write”. Don’t share it — anyone with it can edit the site.
